# 失敗経験2（中古品のオンラインショッピング） - ADER Error ジーンズ

## 日本語

最初は失敗したと思った買い物は、韓国のブランド「ADER Error」のジーンズです。
7年ほど前、大学生の頃に購入しました。当時はおしゃれに夢中で、どうしてもこのブランドのジーンズが欲しかったんです。

当時は今ほど人気ではなく、値段もそこまで高くありませんでした。でも今では有名ブランドになり、今年韓国を訪れた際には、店舗に入るだけで30分以上も並ぶほどでした。

その時、私は自分の腰回りのサイズを正確に分かっておらず、届いたジーンズが全くフィットしなかったんです。チャックを閉めてもズレて見え、かなりショックを受けました。

最初は売ろうかと思いましたが、1年後にオーバーサイズのストリート系ファッションが日本で流行り始めたため、ベルトで何とか履くことにしました。結果的に、それが自分の新しいスタイルにぴったり合ったんです。

今でもそのジーンズを履いていて、韓国の店舗に行った際、スタッフに「それは昔のモデルですよね」と声をかけられ、長年好きでいることを伝えられました。
結局、その時の買い物は失敗ではなく、ファッションの面白さを教えてくれた思い出になりました。

## English

One thing I bought but wasn't happy with at first was a pair of jeans from a Korean brand called *ADER Error*.

It happened about seven years ago when I was a university student, and at that time I was really into fashion and wanted to look stylish.

Back then, the brand wasn't as popular as it is now, and the jeans were relatively affordable like 10,000 yen. But these days, ADER Error has become a famous high-end brand. When I visited Korea this year, I even had to wait in line for more than thirty minutes just to enter the store. It's crazy to think how much it has changed!

Anyway, when I bought the jeans online, I didn't really know my exact waist size. So when they arrived, I was really disappointed to find out that they didn't fit me properly. Even when I zipped them up, they still looked kind of awkward, and I remember feeling quite frustrated.

At that time, I thought about reselling them, but about a year later, oversized and casual street fashion suddenly became really popular in Japan. So instead of selling them, I decided to keep them and started wearing them with a belt. Surprisingly, they ended up matching my new style perfectly.

Now, even after so many years, I still wear them. When I went to Korea recently, one of the store staff noticed that they were an old model and said it was really cool that I had kept them for so long. I told them I'd been a fan of the brand since the early days.

Looking back, I'm glad I didn't give up on that pair, because it reminds me how trends and tastes can change over time.
