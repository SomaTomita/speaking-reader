# 欲しいもの・プレゼント (Things You Want & Gifts)

## Q1. Is there something you really want to buy now?

### 日本語
はい、勉強用に iPad が欲しいです。最近はほとんどのノートをタイプで取っているのですが、手書きの方が記憶に残りやすいと感じています。iPad があれば、集中力も効率も両方向上すると思います。

### English
Yes, I've actually been wanting to buy an iPad for my studies for a while now. These days, I take most of my notes by typing, but I've noticed that handwriting helps me retain information much better. If I had an iPad, I genuinely believe it would boost both my concentration and my overall efficiency when I'm studying.

---

## Q2. Have you received any gifts recently?

### 日本語
はい、最近、彼女がスタンディングデスクをくれました。本当に驚きました。私がどれほど勉強やコーディングに時間を費やしているかを、彼女がよく理解してくれている証拠です。実用的でありながら、とても思いやりのある贈り物だと感じました。

### English
Yes, actually. My girlfriend recently gave me a standing desk, and I was genuinely surprised. It really showed me how well she understands just how much time I spend studying and coding. It was such a thoughtful gift, incredibly practical but also really considerate, because she knew exactly what would improve my daily routine.

---

## Q3. Do you prefer practical or luxury gifts?

### 日本語
私は圧倒的に実用的な贈り物の方が好きです。普段、自分用に高価なものを買わないので、実用的な贈り物をもらうと特別に感じます。贅沢品よりも、生産性を高める道具に投資してもらう方が、ずっと嬉しいです。

### English
I definitely prefer practical gifts over luxury ones, without a doubt. Since I rarely spend money on expensive things for myself, receiving something practical feels extra special. Personally, I'd much rather receive a tool that boosts my productivity than a luxury item. It just feels like a more meaningful way to invest in my everyday life.

---

## Q4. Do you like giving gifts to others?

### 日本語
はい、とても好きです。特に、相手が喜びそうなものを想像しながら選ぶ時間が楽しいです。値段よりも、相手のことをどれだけ考えて選んだかが大切だと思います。

### English
Yes, I really enjoy it. The best part for me is actually the process of thinking carefully about what the other person might like. In my view, the price tag matters far less than how much thought you've put into choosing it. A well-chosen inexpensive gift can mean far more than something expensive that wasn't thought through.
