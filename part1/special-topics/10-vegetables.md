# Vegetables (野菜)

## Q1. Do you think it's easy to grow vegetables?

### 日本語

いいえ、そこまで簡単ではないと思います。良質な土壌、適切な日光、そして定期的な水やりなど、継続的なケアが必要です。虫対策や天候による影響も考えると、意外と手間のかかる作業だと感じます。ただ、自分で育てた新鮮な野菜を収穫できた時の達成感は格別で、その点では十分にやりがいのある活動だと思います。

### English

No, I don't think it's particularly easy. Growing vegetables requires good soil, enough sunlight, and regular care such as watering the plants on a consistent basis. Once you factor in dealing with pests and the impact of the weather, it's actually surprisingly demanding work. Having said that, the sense of achievement when you harvest your own home-grown vegetables is really something special, and in that sense it's definitely a rewarding activity.

---

## Q2. Is growing vegetables popular in your country?

### 日本語

都市部ではそこまで一般的ではありませんが、最近は家庭菜園やベランダ菜園を始める人が少しずつ増えている印象があります。特に健康志向が高まっているので、自分で育てた無農薬の野菜を食べたいという人が多いようです。田舎ではそもそも庭や畑を持っている家庭も多く、自然と家庭菜園の文化が根付いていると思います。

### English

It's not particularly common in urban areas, but I've noticed that more and more people have started home gardens or balcony gardens recently. Since there's a growing awareness around healthy eating, quite a few people seem to want to eat pesticide-free vegetables they've grown themselves. In rural areas, many households already have gardens or small fields, so the home gardening culture has naturally taken root there.

---

## Q3. Should schools teach students how to grow vegetables?

### 日本語

はい、ぜひ教えるべきだと思います。野菜を育てる過程を通じて、子供たちは食べ物がどこから来るのかを実感でき、食への感謝の気持ちが育まれます。また、植物の成長には時間がかかるので、忍耐や責任感を学ぶ良い機会にもなります。教科書だけでは得られない、実践的な学びが得られると思います。

### English

Yes, I genuinely think schools should teach it. By going through the process of growing vegetables, children can really feel where their food actually comes from, which helps develop a sense of gratitude for what's on their plate. On top of that, since plants take time to grow, it's a great opportunity for them to learn patience and responsibility. It offers practical lessons you simply can't get from textbooks alone.

---

## Q4. Do you eat vegetables every day?

### 日本語

はい、できるだけ毎日食べるようにしています。最近は栄養バランスをかなり意識するようになって、特にタンパク質と野菜の組み合わせを大事にしています。例えば、そばに鶏肉や卵と一緒に、ほうれん草やきのこをトッピングすることが多いです。野菜を意識的に摂ることで、体調も明らかに良くなったと実感しています。

### English

Yes, I try to eat vegetables every single day as much as I can. Recently I've become much more conscious of nutritional balance, and I really value combining protein with vegetables properly. For example, I often add things like spinach or mushrooms to my soba along with chicken or eggs. Since I've started being more intentional about my vegetable intake, I've noticed a clear improvement in how I feel day to day.
