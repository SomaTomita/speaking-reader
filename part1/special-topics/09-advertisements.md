# Advertisements (広告)

## Q1. What kind of advertising do you like?

### 日本語
私はクリエイティブでユーモアのある広告が好きです。例えば、短いストーリー仕立てになっていて、商品の特徴を自然に伝えてくれるようなタイプのCMには惹かれます。一方、ただ商品の良さを連呼するような広告は、あまり心に残りません。情報と娯楽のバランスが取れている広告が、一番効果的だと感じます。

### English
I tend to like advertisements that are creative and genuinely funny. For example, I'm really drawn to commercials that are structured like a short story and naturally show the product's features without being too pushy. On the other hand, ads that just repeat how great the product is don't really stick with me at all. In my view, the most effective ads are the ones that balance information and entertainment well.

---

## Q2. Do you like advertisements?

### 日本語
気持ちとしては半々ですね。よく作られた広告は見ていて楽しいし、新しい商品を知るきっかけにもなります。しかし、最近はネット上の広告がかなり多くて、動画の途中や作業中に突然出てくると正直うんざりします。広告そのものよりも、出てくる頻度とタイミングの問題なのかもしれません。

### English
To be honest, I have mixed feelings about them. Well-made adverts can be genuinely fun to watch, and they often introduce me to products I wouldn't have discovered otherwise. However, there are so many ads online these days, and when they suddenly pop up in the middle of a video or while I'm working, I find it quite irritating. I think the real issue is the frequency and timing rather than the ads themselves.

---

## Q3. Is there much advertising in your country?

### 日本語
はい、日本は広告の量が非常に多いと思います。電車の中吊り広告、駅の看板、YouTubeやSNSの広告まで、生活のあらゆる場所で目にします。特に都市部では、どこを見ても何かしらの広告が目に入る感じです。情報量が多すぎて、逆に内容を覚えていないことも多いですが、日本の日常風景の一部になっています。

### English
Yes, I'd say Japan has an enormous amount of advertising. You see adverts hanging inside train carriages, on station signage, and on platforms like YouTube and social media, so they're pretty much everywhere in daily life. Especially in urban areas, it feels like wherever you look, something's being advertised. There's so much information that I often don't remember the content, but it has become part of the everyday scenery.

---

## Q4. Have you ever bought something because of an advertisement?

### 日本語
はい、何度かあります。特にSNSでよく表示されるターゲティング広告は、私の興味にかなり合っているので、そこから生産性ツールや本を買ったことがあります。ただ、衝動買いを避けるために、広告を見てからすぐに買うのではなく、一度調べてから判断するようにしています。その習慣のおかげで、後悔の少ない買い物ができていると思います。

### English
Yes, a few times actually. Targeted ads on social media in particular tend to match my interests remarkably well, so I've ended up buying productivity tools and books through them. However, to avoid impulse purchases, I make it a rule not to buy straight after seeing an ad, and I always do a bit of research first. Thanks to that habit, I reckon I've managed to minimise purchases I later regret.
