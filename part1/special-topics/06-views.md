# Views (景色)

## Q1. Do you like taking pictures of different views?

### 日本語
はい、特に京都のお寺や古い建物を見たときには、つい写真を撮りたくなります。自宅の近くにある東寺の五重塔は特に写真映えするので、季節ごとに違った表情を楽しめます。ただ、撮ることに集中しすぎると、逆にその瞬間を楽しめなくなることもあるので、時々はカメラを置いて景色そのものを味わうようにしています。

### English
Yes, especially when I see temples or old buildings in Kyoto, I often find myself reaching for my phone to take a photo. The five-storey pagoda at To-ji Temple near my house is particularly photogenic, and I can enjoy how it changes across the seasons. That said, if I focus too much on capturing it, I sometimes lose the chance to actually enjoy the moment, so I occasionally put my phone away and just soak it in.

---

## Q2. Do you prefer views in urban areas or rural areas?

### 日本語
私は断然、田舎の景色の方が好きです。普段は勉強やコーディングで画面をずっと見ているので、山や川、田んぼの広がりといった自然の景色を見ると、本当に心がリセットされる感覚があります。京都は盆地で山に囲まれているので、少し郊外に出ればすぐに自然に触れられるのも気に入っています。

### English
I'd definitely say I prefer views in rural areas. Since I usually spend my time staring at a screen while studying or coding, seeing things like mountains, rivers or stretches of rice fields really helps me mentally reset in a way nothing else does. One thing I appreciate about Kyoto is that it sits in a basin surrounded by mountains, so I can escape into nature without having to travel very far at all.

---

## Q3. Do you prefer views in your own country or in other countries?

### 日本語
両方に魅力があると思いますが、未知への好奇心という意味では、海外の景色の方が刺激的に感じます。いつかアマゾンの熱帯雨林の景色を見てみたいです。自然の生息地で生きる動物たちの迫力は、日本ではなかなか味わえないと思います。ただ、四季がはっきりしている日本の景色も、改めて見ると本当に美しいです。

### English
Both have their own appeal, but in terms of curiosity for the unknown, I'd say views abroad feel more exciting to me. One day I'd genuinely love to see the Amazon rainforest with my own eyes. The raw power of animals living in their natural habitat is something you just can't really experience in Japan. Having said that, Japan's scenery with its four distinct seasons is genuinely stunning when you take a fresh look.

---

## Q4. Is there a view near your home that you particularly like?

### 日本語
はい、東寺の五重塔の景色が一番好きです。自宅から歩いて行ける距離にあって、夕方に通ると特に美しく、夕焼けと木造建築のコントラストが絶妙です。子供の頃からずっと見てきた景色ですが、今でも見るたびに感動します。地元の人間として、この景色が身近にあるのは本当に幸せなことだと感じます。

### English
Yes, the view of the five-storey pagoda at To-ji Temple is my absolute favourite. It's within walking distance from my house, and when I pass by in the evening, it looks especially beautiful because of the contrast between the sunset and the wooden architecture. I've been seeing this view since I was a child, but it still moves me every single time. As a local, having it so close to home feels like a real privilege.
