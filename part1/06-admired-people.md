# 尊敬する人・会ってみたい有名人 (Admired People & Celebrities)

## Q1. Who do you admire the most?

### 日本語
私が尊敬している人物の一人は、渋沢栄一です。彼を尊敬するのは、ビジネスは単に利益を生み出すだけでなく、社会に奉仕すべきだと信じていたからです。彼は留学してヨーロッパから学び、その考えを日本の近代化に応用しました。このような哲学は、今の時代にも非常に重要だと感じます。

### English
One person I genuinely admire is Eiichi Shibusawa, a Japanese businessman from the Meiji era. What I respect most about him is his belief that business should do more than just make profits, and that it should contribute to society as a whole. He studied in Europe and later applied those ideas to help modernise Japan, and I think that kind of philosophy is still incredibly relevant today.

---

## Q2. What would you ask him if you could meet him?

### 日本語
もし会えるなら、彼がどうやって倫理と経済成長を両立させたのかを、ぜひ聞いてみたいです。現代でも、ビジネスと倫理のバランスを取るのは非常に難しい問題です。彼の時代の日本でどのようにそれを実現したのか、とても興味があります。

### English
If I could meet him, I'd really love to ask how he managed to balance ethics and economic growth. Even today, finding that balance between profitability and doing the right thing is incredibly challenging for most companies. I'd be genuinely curious to hear how he pulled it off during such a rapidly changing period in Japanese history.

---

## Q3. Is there a celebrity you would like to meet?

### 日本語
かつてサッカーをやっていた者として、プロのサッカー選手に会いたいです。具体的には、アンドレス・イニエスタ選手です。彼のプレースタイルは芸術的で、いつも感動していました。できれば一度でいいから、彼とパス交換をしてみたいです。

### English
As someone who used to play football quite seriously, I'd absolutely love to meet a professional footballer. Specifically, Andrés Iniesta would be my top choice. His playing style is almost artistic, and I've always been deeply moved by watching him play. Honestly, if I could just exchange a few passes with him, even once, it would be a dream come true.

---

## Q4. What would you want to learn from him?

### 日本語
彼の長いキャリアの中で、どうやって規律とモチベーションを維持してきたのか、ぜひ聞いてみたいです。トップレベルで何年も活躍するには、単に才能だけでは不十分だと思います。精神的な強さや日々の習慣について、彼から学べることは多いはずです。

### English
What I'd most want to learn from him is how he's maintained his discipline and motivation throughout such a long career. Staying at the top level for so many years takes far more than just talent, I reckon. I'm sure there's a huge amount to learn from him about mental toughness and the daily routines that kept him going at the elite level.
