# 好きな天気 (Favourite Weather)

## Q1. What kind of weather do you like?

### 日本語

晴れの日が一番好きです。気分が上がりますし、外を散歩したりジムに行ったりするのも楽しくなります。あと、晴れの日は髪がまとまりやすいので、それだけでもありがたいですね。

### English

Sunny days are my favourite, without a doubt. They just put me in a good mood, and I feel more motivated to go for a walk or hit the gym. Plus, my hair behaves much better when it's dry out, which is a bonus.

---

## Q2. What is the weather like in your hometown?

### 日本語

京都は盆地で周りを山に囲まれているので、熱や湿気がこもりやすいんです。だから夏はものすごく蒸し暑くて、冬もかなり冷え込みます。春と秋は過ごしやすくて、観光客も一番多い時期ですね。個人的には秋の京都が一番好きです。

### English

I'm from Kyoto, and it's actually in a basin surrounded by mountains, so the heat and humidity tend to get trapped. That makes summers extremely hot and muggy, and winters quite bitter as well. Spring and autumn are the most pleasant seasons, and that's when we get the most tourists. Personally, I think autumn in Kyoto is the best time of year.

---

## Q3. Does weather affect how you feel?

### 日本語

かなり影響されますね。晴れの日は自然とやる気が出るんですけど、雨の日はちょっと憂鬱になります。特に私はくせ毛がひどくて、雨の日は髪が爆発するんです。朝どれだけセットしても意味がなくなるので、それだけでテンションが下がります。イギリスでは日照時間が短いせいで気分が落ち込みやすい人が多いと聞いたことがあるので、天気の影響って本当に大きいんだなと思います。

### English

Quite a lot, actually. On sunny days I feel naturally energised, but rainy days tend to bring my mood down a bit. The main reason is that I have really curly hair, and whenever it rains, my hair just goes completely out of control. No matter how much time I spend styling it in the morning, it's all ruined the moment I step outside, which is honestly frustrating. I've also heard that in the UK, people are more prone to feeling low because of the shorter daylight hours, so I think weather really does have a big impact on how we feel.

---

## Q4. Do you check the weather forecast often?

### 日本語

はい、毎朝チェックしています。特に雨が降るかどうかは重要で、雨なら帽子をかぶるか、髪をどうするか考えないといけないので。あとは週末にジムに行くか外でランニングするかを決めるときにも見ますね。

### English

Yes, I check it every morning. I've become especially mindful of whether it's going to rain, because then I need to decide whether to wear a hat or figure out what to do with my hair. I also check it at weekends to decide whether to go to the gym or run outside instead.
