# 友人・人間関係 (Friends & Relationships)

## Q1. Do you have a best friend? How did you meet?

### 日本語
はい、います。大学生の時に焼肉店でアルバイトをしていた時に出会いました。最初は二人とも歌が好きだったので、よく一緒にカラオケに行くようになり、そこから自然と仲良くなっていきました。

### English
Yes, I do have one close friend who I'd say is my best mate. We first met while working part-time at a Korean barbecue restaurant during our university days. We bonded over our shared love of singing, so we started going to karaoke together pretty often, and our friendship just naturally grew from there.

---

## Q2. What kind of person is your best friend?

### 日本語
彼は学業で秀でるタイプではありませんが、非常に頭の回転が速く、決断力に優れています。例えば一緒に買い物に行くと、店内の効率的なルートをほぼ瞬時に計画できます。また、ユーモアのセンスも抜群で、一緒にいてとても楽しい人です。

### English
He's not the type who excels academically, but he's incredibly quick-witted and decisive. For example, whenever we go shopping together, he can plan out the most efficient route through the shop almost instantly. On top of that, he has a sharp sense of humour, especially his witty takes on current trends, so spending time with him is always genuinely fun.

---

## Q3. What do you have in common with your best friend?

### 日本語
彼の美意識には本当に感心させられます。服のセンスも、音楽の趣味も、文化全般に対する見識も抜群です。彼と出会う前の私は、ファッションには全く無頓着でした。でも彼を通じて、服で自分を表現する楽しさや、K-POPからラップまで幅広い音楽を知ることができました。そのため、よく「瓜二つ」と言われます。

### English
What really strikes me about him is his great sense of aesthetics. His fashion, his taste in music, and his cultural knowledge are all impressive. Before I met him, I was completely clueless about fashion. But through him, I learned how to express myself through clothes and discovered a wide range of music from K-pop to rap. Because of this mutual influence, people often say we're like "two peas in a pod".

---

## Q4. How often do you see your best friend?

### 日本語
お互い働き始めてからも、定期的に連絡を取り合っています。ただ、実際に会うのはだいたい3ヶ月に1回ぐらいです。毎年続けている習慣として、年末に一緒に買い物に行き、その後カラオケに行くのがあって、私はそれをとても楽しみにしています。

### English
Even though we're both busy with work these days, we still keep in touch regularly. However, we only actually meet up in person around once every three months or so. There's one tradition we've kept going for years. At the end of each year, we go shopping together and then head to karaoke afterwards, and that's something I always really look forward to.

---

## Q5. Do you prefer spending time with a few close friends or a big group?

### 日本語
私はどちらかというと、少人数の親しい友人と過ごす方が好きです。その方が深い話ができるし、よりリラックスできるからです。大人数の集まりも楽しいのですが、時々少し疲れてしまうこともあります。

### English
Personally, I lean towards spending time with just a few close friends rather than a big group. I find I can have much deeper conversations that way, and it feels a lot more relaxing. Don't get me wrong, big gatherings can be fun too, but sometimes I find them a bit draining, especially after a long week at work.
