# よく使う交通手段 (Transport)

## Q1. How do you usually travel around?

### 日本語

普段は主にバスで通勤しています。京都には地下鉄もありますが、バスの方が職場に直接行けるので便利です。ただ、京都は世界的に有名な観光地なので、特に朝の時間帯は観光客でバスがすごく混んでいて、それだけは少し大変です。

### English

I mainly take the bus to get to work. Kyoto does have a subway system, but the bus gets me closer to my workplace, so it's just more practical. The downside is that Kyoto is a world-famous tourist destination, so the buses can get really packed, especially in the morning, since tourists are often heading out to sightseeing spots at the same time.

---

## Q2. What is the most popular means of transport in your country?

### 日本語

日本全体で見ると、電車が最も一般的な交通手段だと思います。特に都市部では正確で速くて本当に頼りになります。ただ京都に限って言えば、バスネットワークが充実していて観光スポットに直接アクセスできるので、観光客には特に人気があります。

### English

Nationwide, I think trains are by far the most popular form of transport. Especially in urban areas, they're incredibly reliable and punctual. That said, in Kyoto specifically, the bus network is very well developed since it connects directly to most tourist attractions, so it tends to be the go-to option for visitors in particular.

---

## Q3. Do you prefer public transport or driving?

### 日本語

基本的には公共交通機関の方が好きです。京都の市街地は道も細くて駐車場も少ないので、車を運転するのは正直あまり気が進みません。バスは混むこともありますが、乗ってしまえばスマホを見たり本を読んだりできるので、その時間を有効に使えるのが気に入っています。

### English

I generally prefer public transport. The streets in central Kyoto are quite narrow and parking is very limited, so driving honestly doesn't appeal to me that much. The buses can get crowded, but once I'm on, I can read or check my phone, so I actually don't mind the commute time. It feels more productive than sitting behind the wheel.

---

## Q4. Has public transport in your area improved?

### 日本語

正直、改善されているとは言いにくい部分もあります。近年、京都を訪れる観光客の数が急増していて、バスが以前より格段に混雑するようになりました。地元の人間からすると、通勤のバスに乗れないこともあって少し困ります。専用の観光ルートを設けるなど対策も進んでいますが、まだ十分ではないと感じています。

### English

Honestly, it's hard to say it's improved, at least from a local's perspective. The number of tourists visiting Kyoto has grown dramatically in recent years, and the buses have become far more crowded than they used to be. There are times when I genuinely can't get on my usual bus because it's already full of sightseers. Some measures have been put in place, like dedicated tourist routes, but personally I don't think it's been enough yet.
