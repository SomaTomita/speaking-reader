# レストラン・好きな食べ物 (Restaurants & Favourite Food)

## Q1. Do you often go to restaurants?

### 日本語
はい、私はラーメン店によく行く方で、特に手早く腹ごしらえをしたい時に利用することが多いです。学生時代とは違い、今は食事の質やバランスをより意識するようになりました。そのため、比較的あっさりしたメニューや、トッピングを自由に選べるお店を選ぶようにしています。

### English
Yes, I'm someone who goes to ramen shops quite often, especially when I just want to grab a quick bite. Unlike my student days, I'm now much more conscious of the quality and balance of my meals. Because of that, I tend to pick restaurants with lighter menu options, or ones where I can freely choose my own toppings to suit my needs.

---

## Q2. What kind of food do you like?

### 日本語
私の好きな食べ物はそばで、自分の食の好みに合っているため、かなり頻繁に食べています。最近は、単に味だけで食べ物を選ぶのではなく、栄養価、特にタンパク質の摂取量を考慮するようになりました。そばは比較的ヘルシーで、高タンパク質の食材と組み合わせやすいことが、私がそばを好む主な理由の一つです。

### English
My favourite food is soba noodles, which I eat pretty frequently because it really suits my taste. Lately, I've started choosing food based not just on taste, but also on nutritional value, particularly protein intake. One of the main reasons I love soba is that it's relatively healthy and pairs really well with high-protein ingredients, which fits perfectly with my current lifestyle goals.

---

## Q3. Do you cook at home?

### 日本語
はい、時々料理をします。栄養価の高い食事を意識しているので、鶏肉や卵、豆腐などをトッピングしたそばをよく作ります。そうすることで、栄養価が高く、満足感のある食事になります。

### English
Yes, I cook at home from time to time. Since I try to pay attention to the nutritional value of what I eat, I often make soba topped with things like chicken, eggs or tofu. That combination ends up being really nutritious and surprisingly satisfying, so it's become my go-to when I want something healthy but still quick and genuinely enjoyable.

---

## Q4. Has your attitude towards food changed?

### 日本語
はい、かなり変わりました。昔は好きなものを好きなだけ食べていましたが、今は健康全般をサポートするような選択を心がけるようになりました。この経験を通じて、食事は楽しみながら機能性も兼ね備えられるものだと学びました。

### English
Yes, it's changed quite significantly. I used to just eat whatever I felt like, in whatever quantity, but now I try to make choices that support my overall health. Through this experience, I've really come to appreciate that meals can be both enjoyable and functional at the same time, and you don't have to sacrifice one for the other, which was a nice realisation.

---

## Q5. Do you prefer eating out or eating at home?

### 日本語
基本的には家で食べる方が好きです。自分で食材を選べるので、より健康的な食事ができるからです。ただし、友人と会う時や特別な日には、外食で雰囲気を楽しむのも良いと思います。

### English
I generally prefer eating at home. The main reason is that I can choose my own ingredients, which means I can enjoy healthier meals overall. That said, when I'm catching up with friends or celebrating something special, eating out is a lovely experience because you get to enjoy the atmosphere of the restaurant, which is something you simply can't replicate at home.
