# よく買い物をする場所 (Shopping Places)

## Q1. Where do you usually go shopping?

### 日本語

普段は河原町という京都の中心部のショッピングエリアによく行きます。家からバスで30分ほどの場所です。特に古着屋を巡るのが好きで、パート2でも話した友人とよく一緒に行きます。彼はファッションのセンスが抜群なので、一緒に回るとすごく楽しいんです。

### English

I usually go to Kawaramachi, which is a big shopping area in central Kyoto, about thirty minutes from my house by bus. I'm especially into browsing vintage clothing stores, and I often go with a friend of mine who has a great sense of style. Wandering around the shops with him is honestly one of my favourite things to do.

---

## Q2. Do you prefer shopping online or in physical stores?

### 日本語

断然、実店舗の方が好きです。実は以前、オンラインで韓国ブランドのジーンズを買ったときにサイズが全く合わなくて、けっこう失敗した経験があるんです。だから特に服に関しては、やっぱり試着して、実際に手に取ってから選びたいと思っています。

### English

I definitely prefer physical stores. Actually, I once bought a pair of jeans from a Korean brand online, and they didn't fit me at all, so it was quite a disappointing experience. Since then, when it comes to clothes especially, I really want to try things on and see them in person before I buy.

---

## Q3. What do you enjoy shopping for the most?

### 日本語

やっぱり古着が一番ですね。中古品なので値段が手頃なのも好きな理由のひとつですが、それ以上に、古着屋では大体どれも残り1点なので、今持っている服にドンピシャで合う1着を見つけたときは本当に嬉しいんです。あとは最近スニーカーにハマっていて、どこに行っても在庫がなかったのに、たまたま自分のサイズだけ残っていたとき、ああいう瞬間が一番テンションが上がりますね。

### English

Vintage clothing is definitely my favourite. Part of the appeal is that it's secondhand, so it's quite affordable, but more than that, at thrift shops there's usually only one of each item left, so when I find the perfect piece that matches something I already own, it feels really special. I've also been into sneakers lately, and there have been times when a pair was sold out everywhere, but the shop just happened to have my size left. Those little moments of luck are the most exciting part for me.

---

## Q4. Has your shopping habit changed over the years?

### 日本語

はい、かなり変わりました。一番買い物をしていたのは大学生の頃で、それと比べると今は服やスニーカーをあまり買わなくなりました。今はエンジニアなので、マウスやキーボード、性能の良いパソコンといったガジェット系にお金を使うことが多いです。ただ全体的には、近年の物価高もあって消費はかなり控えめになりました。日本は30年前、世界一のGDPを誇っていたので、今の状況と比べると、少し謙虚な気持ちにさせられますね。

### English

Yes, it's changed quite a lot. I bought the most when I was a university student, and compared to back then, I hardly buy clothes or sneakers anymore. These days I'm an engineer, so I tend to spend more on gadgets, things like a mouse, a keyboard, or a high-spec laptop. That said, I've generally become much more careful with my spending, partly because of how much prices have gone up in recent years. Japan actually had the world's largest GDP about thirty years ago, so comparing that with where we are now makes me feel rather humble about it.
