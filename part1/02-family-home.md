# 家族・家 (Family & Home)

## Q1. Can you tell me about your family?

### 日本語
私の家族は4人の核家族で、父、母、兄、そして私です。兄は30代で、今は別のところに住んでいます。私は両親と一緒に住んでいます。家族でテレビを見ながらリラックスする時間が大好きで、よくいろんな話をしています。

### English
We're a pretty typical nuclear family of four. There's my dad, my mum, my older brother and me. My brother's in his thirties now and has moved out, so it's just me living with my parents at the moment. One thing I really enjoy is relaxing in front of the TV with them, and we tend to chat quite a lot while we're watching.

---

## Q2. Do you have any family traditions?

### 日本語
はい、あります。毎年お正月にいとこたちも集まって、合計で10人ほどで世界中のビールを飲み比べるのが恒例になっています。これは家族の伝統のようなもので、みんなが楽しみにしているイベントです。

### English
Yes, we actually have quite a fun one. Every New Year, my cousins come over and around ten of us get together to try beers from all over the world, and it's basically become a family tradition. Everyone really looks forward to it, and it's a great way for us to catch up once a year.

---

## Q3. Who do you respect the most in your family?

### 日本語
私が最も尊敬しているのは母です。彼女は常に私を支えてくれる姿勢を持っています。大きな決断やリスクのある挑戦について話しても、決して否定的な態度を取ったことがありません。ドイツに行きたいと言った時も、交換留学プログラムに応募した時も、励まし、背中を押してくれました。

### English
The person I respect most is probably my mum. What I admire most is her unwavering supportive attitude. Whenever I talk about big decisions or risky challenges, she's never once been negative about it. For instance, when I said I wanted to move to Germany, or when I applied for an exchange programme, she always encouraged me and had my back.

---

## Q4. Can you describe the home you live in?

### 日本語
3階建ての一戸建てに住んでいます。日本の伝統的な造りで、玄関があるスタイルです。部屋はそこまで広くはありませんが、収納スペースがたくさんあるのが気に入っています。駅からは徒歩で20分ほどありますが、自転車なら5〜7分で着くので、そこまで不便ではありません。

### English
I live in a three-storey detached house built in a fairly traditional Japanese style, with a proper entrance area called a "genkan". The rooms themselves aren't particularly spacious, but there's plenty of storage space, which I really appreciate. It's about a twenty-minute walk from the nearest station, but I can cycle there in five to seven minutes, so it's not too inconvenient.

---

## Q5. Is the area around your home quiet?

### 日本語
はい、わりと静かで穏やかな雰囲気の地域です。騒音はあまりありません。むしろ子供の頃、私がサッカーの練習をしていた時の方が、家の前で騒音を出していたかもしれません(笑)。今は落ち着いた環境で暮らせています。

### English
Yes, it's a pretty peaceful neighbourhood, to be honest. There isn't much noise at all. If anything, I was probably the one making noise back when I was a kid practising football in front of the house. These days, though, it's a nice and calm environment, which I really appreciate, especially when I need to concentrate on studying.

---

## Q6. Is there anything you would like to change about your home?

### 日本語
一つだけ改善してほしいのは、私の部屋が3階にあることです。何か忘れ物をすると、階段を2つ登り直さなければならず、かなり疲れるからです。もう少し上の階と下の階の距離が近いと、もっと便利だと思います。

### English
There's actually one thing I'd love to change. My bedroom is on the third floor, which can be a bit of a pain. Whenever I forget something, I have to climb up two flights of stairs again, and it gets pretty exhausting, especially when I'm tired. If my room were on a lower floor, things would be much more convenient.
