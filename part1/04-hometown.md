# ホームタウン・住んでいる地域 (Hometown)

## Q1. Where is your hometown?

### 日本語
私のホームタウンは京都で、京都で生まれ京都で育ちました。京都は日本で最も有名な観光地の一つで、世界中から多くの人が訪れます。伝統的なお寺がたくさんある一方、最先端のファッションを楽しむための買い物もできる、魅力的な都市です。

### English
My hometown is Kyoto, where I was both born and raised. Kyoto is one of the most famous tourist destinations in Japan, and it attracts visitors from all over the world. What I love about it is the balance. You can explore countless traditional temples, and at the same time, enjoy shopping for the latest fashion trends.

---

## Q2. How long have you lived in your hometown?

### 日本語
ほぼずっと京都に住んでいます。正確には24年間です。仕事の関係で2年間だけ横浜に住んでいましたが、それ以外はずっと京都にいます。横浜は東京の近くで、都会の慌ただしさや活気がすごかったですが、やはり京都の落ち着いた雰囲気が自分には合っています。

### English
I've lived in Kyoto for most of my life, twenty-four years to be exact. The only exception was a two-year period when I moved to Yokohama for work. Yokohama is close to Tokyo and it had that real big-city buzz, but ultimately I feel that Kyoto, with its more laid-back atmosphere, suits me much better.

---

## Q3. What do you like most about your hometown?

### 日本語
京都の一番好きなところは、全てがちょうど良いバランスで存在していることです。伝統と現代性、自然と都市の便利さが共存しています。どこか特別な場所に行きたいと思ったら、古いお寺でも、おしゃれなカフェでも、少し歩けば見つかるのがとても気に入っています。

### English
What I appreciate most about Kyoto is how well-balanced everything is. Tradition and modernity, nature and urban convenience, they all coexist really nicely. Whenever I feel like going somewhere special, whether it's an ancient temple or a trendy little café, it's usually just a short walk away, which I genuinely love about living there.

---

## Q4. Are there any problems in your hometown?

### 日本語
はい、観光が人気になりすぎて、オーバーツーリズムによる騒音や満員電車といった問題が起きています。私もバスに乗る時に少し不便に感じることがあります。また、京都は盆地で山に囲まれているため、一度寒くなったり暑くなったりすると、その状態が長く続くのもイライラします。夏は何もしていなくても汗をかきます。

### English
Yes, unfortunately. Tourism has become so popular that over-tourism is causing issues like noise and overcrowded buses and trains. I personally find it a bit frustrating when I'm trying to commute. On top of that, because Kyoto sits in a basin surrounded by mountains, once it gets hot or cold, the weather tends to stay that way for ages. In summer, you start sweating just standing still.

---

## Q5. Would you recommend your hometown to tourists?

### 日本語
はい、もちろんおすすめします。ただし、訪れる時期を選んだ方が良いと思います。桜や紅葉のシーズンは最も美しいですが、観光客も非常に多いので、少し時期をずらすと、より京都の本来の魅力をゆっくり味わえると思います。

### English
Absolutely, I'd definitely recommend it. Having said that, I'd suggest choosing the timing carefully. Cherry blossom and autumn foliage seasons are the most stunning, but they also draw massive crowds. If tourists came slightly off-season, they'd be able to enjoy the authentic charm of Kyoto at a much more relaxed pace, which I think is a far better experience.
