# 日本の有名な行事・好きな文化 (Japanese Events & Culture)

## Q1. What is a famous event or festival in your country?

### 日本語

日本で最も有名な行事の一つは、春に行う「花見」だと思います。桜の木の下に友人や家族と集まって、お弁当やお酒を楽しみながら、ピンク色の花びらをゆっくりと眺める文化です。桜の季節はわずか1〜2週間しか続かないので、その儚さもまた日本人にとって特別な意味を持っています。海外からの観光客にも非常に人気があります。

### English

One of the most famous events in Japan is hanami, which means cherry blossom viewing in spring. It's a tradition where people gather under cherry trees with friends or family, enjoy bento boxes and drinks, and slowly take in the pink petals falling around them. The season only lasts one or two weeks, so that brief, fleeting nature actually holds a special meaning for Japanese people, and it's become incredibly popular with tourists from abroad.

---

## Q2. Do you enjoy celebrating these events?

### 日本語

はい、とても楽しんでいます。特に京都に住んでいるので、花見に関してはかなり恵まれた環境にあります。自宅の近くにも有名な桜の名所がいくつかあって、毎年家族や彼女とお弁当を持って出かけます。桜の季節が来ると「一年が巡ってきたな」と感じられて、日本人らしい季節感を味わえる特別な瞬間だと思います。

### English

Yes, I genuinely enjoy it. Since I live in Kyoto, I'm actually in quite a privileged position when it comes to hanami. There are several famous cherry blossom spots within walking distance of my house, and every year I head out with my family or girlfriend with a packed bento. When the season comes around, I feel that another year has come full circle, and it's a special moment where I can really appreciate the Japanese sense of the seasons.

---

## Q3. Are there any other famous events you would recommend?

### 日本語

はい、京都の「祇園祭」は絶対におすすめします。7月に1ヶ月間にわたって行われる日本三大祭りの一つで、豪華な山鉾と呼ばれる巨大な山車が街を練り歩きます。千年以上の歴史があり、伝統工芸や音楽、屋台の食べ物を一度に楽しめます。外国人の友人を連れて行くと、必ず感動してくれる、本物の日本文化を体験できるお祭りです。

### English

Yes, I'd highly recommend the Gion Matsuri in Kyoto. It's one of Japan's three biggest festivals, running throughout July, and it features these gorgeous massive floats called yamaboko being pulled through the streets. With over a thousand years of history behind it, you can enjoy traditional crafts, music and street food all in one place. Whenever I take foreign friends, they're always blown away, and it's a proper chance to experience authentic Japanese culture.

---

## Q4. What aspect of your culture do you like most?

### 日本語

私が一番好きな日本文化の側面は「おもてなし」の心です。お客様を大切にし、相手の立場を想像して行動するという考え方は、レストランからコンビニまで、日常のあらゆる場面に息づいています。また、四季それぞれに異なる行事や食べ物があって、季節の移ろいを丁寧に楽しむ感覚も、日本らしくて素敵だと感じます。伝統と現代性が共存しているのも、京都出身の私には特に魅力的に映ります。

### English

The aspect of Japanese culture I love most is the spirit of omotenashi, which is our way of genuinely caring for guests by imagining what they might need before they ask. This mindset is alive everywhere, from high-end restaurants to convenience stores. On top of that, each of the four seasons has its own events and foods, and I love how we savour the subtle changes throughout the year. As someone from Kyoto, I also find it fascinating how tradition and modernity coexist so naturally.

---

## Q5. Do you think traditional events are still important today?

### 日本語

はい、非常に重要だと思います。時代が変わっても、伝統行事は世代をつなぐ大切な役割を果たしています。例えば、お正月に家族で集まって年越しそばを食べる習慣は、どれだけ忙しい現代でも続いていて、家族の絆を再確認する時間になっています。グローバル化が進むからこそ、自分たちのルーツを大切にする意味が、かえって大きくなっていると感じます。

### English

Yes, I think they're incredibly important. Even as times change, traditional events play a key role in connecting generations. For instance, the custom of gathering with family on New Year's Eve to eat toshikoshi soba still carries on even in today's busy lifestyle, and it gives us a moment to reaffirm our family bonds. If anything, as globalisation moves forward, the meaning of valuing our own roots has actually become even greater, in my view.
