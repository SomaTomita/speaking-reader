# Part 1 High-Score Phrases & Grammar (Band 7-9)

> Part 1 = 4-5 min warm-up. 2-4 sentences per answer. Natural & conversational, NOT formal or rehearsed.

---

## 1. Grammar Structures (Grammatical Range & Accuracy)

### Tense Variety

| Structure                      | Example                                                 | When to Use                  |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Present Perfect**            | I've always been drawn to orange.                       | Experience, duration, change |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | I've been getting into vintage clothing lately.         | Ongoing activity + duration  |
| **Used to / Would**            | I used to play football every weekend.                  | Past habits no longer true   |
| **First Conditional**          | If the weather's nice, I'll probably go for a run.      | Likely future                |
| **Second Conditional**         | If I had more free time, I'd travel more.               | Hypothetical                 |
| **Past Perfect** (sparingly)   | By the time I moved, I'd already lived in three cities. | Background in past stories   |

### Complex Sentence Types

| Structure                 | Example                                                       |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Relative clause**       | It's the kind of place that makes you feel relaxed.           |
| **Non-defining relative** | My hometown, which is in a basin, gets incredibly hot.        |
| **Participle clause**     | Growing up in Kyoto, I got used to hot summers.               |
| **Cleft sentence**        | What I really enjoy is trying new recipes.                    |
| **Adverbial clause**      | Although I enjoy cooking, I rarely have time during the week. |
| **Comparative**           | I'm far more productive in the morning than in the evening.   |

### Natural Grammar Chunks

- I tend to [verb]...
- I'm the kind of person who...
- It depends on...
- I wouldn't say I'm too fussy about it, but...
- I find it [adj] to [verb]...
- I try to [verb] whenever I can.

### Avoid in Part 1 (too formal / sounds rehearsed)

- Third Conditional (unless specifically about past regrets)
- Passive voice ("It is widely believed that...")
- Inversion ("Not only do I enjoy...")
- Overly formal cleft ("It was in 2015 that I first...")

### Natural Idiomatic Expressions

**Safe to use (natural):**

- to be honest / to tell you the truth
- once in a blue moon
- time flies
- at the end of the day

## 3. Discourse Markers (Fluency & Coherence)

### Fillers (replace "um" / "uh")

- Well, ...
- Let me think...
- That's a good question...
- Actually, ...
- I mean, ...

### Hedging (show nuance)

- I'd say...
- I suppose...
- I reckon...
- probably / I guess
- It kind of depends on...
- not necessarily

### Attitude Markers

- to be honest / honestly
- frankly
- unfortunately / thankfully / luckily
- surprisingly / funnily enough
- in fact / as a matter of fact

### Adding Information

- on top of that
- not only that, but...
- plus (casual, natural)
- another thing is...
- what's more

### Contrasting

- having said that / that said
- on the other hand
- although / even though
- but then again
- mind you (British, natural)

### Cause & Result

- so (most natural in Part 1)
- that's why...
- because of that...
- which means...

### Giving Examples

- for instance
- say, ... (very conversational)
- like (informal but natural)
- such as

### Wrapping Up

- so yeah, that's basically why...
- all in all
- basically

> Warning: Don't start every sentence with "Well," or "Actually." One or two discourse markers per answer.

## 4. Pronunciation Tips

### Always Use Contractions

I'm, I've, I'd, it's, that's, there's, doesn't, don't, can't, won't

Full forms sound robotic. Exception: emphasis ("I DO enjoy it").

### Intonation Patterns

**Lists** (rise on each item, fall on last):
"I like Italian, Thai, and Japanese food."

**Contrast** (rise on first, fall on second):
"On sunny days I feel great, but rainy days bring my mood down."

**Attitude markers** (rise, pause, then answer):
"Funnily enough, ..." / "To be honest, ..."

### Sentence Stress

Stress content words, reduce function words:
"I've ALWAYS been DRAWN to ORANGE."
"It PUTS me in a GOOD MOOD."

## 5. Response Patterns by Question Type

### Yes/No + Expand

**Pattern:** Direct answer -> Reason -> Detail

> Do you enjoy cooking?
> "Yes, I really enjoy it, actually. It helps me unwind after a long day, and I've been experimenting with Thai recipes lately."

### Frequency

**Pattern:** Frequency -> Activity -> Reason

> How often do you exercise?
> "I try to hit the gym three or four times a week. It's a great way to clear my mind after work."

### Preference

**Pattern:** State preference -> Why -> Brief contrast

> Coffee or tea?
> "I'd say I slightly prefer coffee, especially first thing in the morning. Having said that, I do enjoy green tea in the afternoon."

### Change Over Time

**Pattern:** Past ("used to") -> Present -> Reason for change

> Has your taste in music changed?
> "Quite a lot, actually. I used to listen to pop in high school, but these days I'm more into jazz. I think as I've gotten older, I've come to appreciate more relaxing music."

### Why Questions

**Pattern:** Direct answer -> Because -> Supporting detail

> Why is your hometown special?
> "I suppose it's because I grew up there and all my childhood memories are tied to it. On top of that, Kyoto has such a rich cultural heritage."

### "It Depends" Questions

**Pattern:** "It depends on..." -> Two scenarios -> Brief conclusion

> Do you prefer staying in or going out?
> "Honestly, it depends on my mood. If I've had a hectic week, I'd rather stay in. But if the weather's nice, I love getting out and exploring."

## Key Warnings

1. **Do NOT memorize full answers.** Prepare ideas and phrases, then adapt in real time.
2. **Do NOT be overly formal.** "It is widely acknowledged..." is Part 3 language. Say: "I try to work out because it helps me feel better."
3. **Do NOT overuse discourse markers.** 1-2 per response is natural.
4. **Do NOT give 1-word answers or 5-sentence essays.** 2-4 sentences is the sweet spot.
5. **Do NOT use rare vocabulary just to impress.** "Invigorating" = fine. "Pulchritudinous" = not fine.
